# Map Design: Bloody Thicket

## Overview

**Location:** Pine Forest  
**Map Type:** Combat / Investigation  
**Grid:** Required  
**Primary Use:** Initial Encounter + Final Battle  
**Recommended Orientation:** Landscape

The Bloody Thicket is a section of snowy pine forest where Ysilwen was ambushed by Lotorrix and his kobold followers.

The same map is used twice during the adventure:

1. The party investigates the aftermath of the first battle.
2. The party returns with Ysilwen for the final confrontation.

The map should therefore feel like a natural wilderness location rather than a purpose-built arena.

---

# Design Goals

The map should communicate:

- A dense snowy pine forest.
- Evidence of a massive dragon battle.
- Uneven and damaged terrain.
- Enough open space for dragon combat.
- Enough cover for kobolds.
- Multiple movement routes.
- Verticality without making the map difficult to read.
- A clear central battlefield.

The map should support both **small-creature combat** and **large-creature combat**.

---

# Recommended Dimensions

**Grid:** 24 × 18 squares

**Recommended Battlemap Size:** 1200 × 900 px minimum

### Scale

**1 square = 5 ft**

### Play Area

Approximately:

**120 ft × 90 ft**

This provides enough space for:

- 10 Kobold Warriors
- 4 Cultist Fanatics
- Lotorrix
- Ysilwen
- Player Characters

without making the battlefield unnecessarily huge.

---

# Layout

## General Shape

The map is an irregular forest clearing surrounded by dense pine trees.

The center contains the remains of the original battle.

The northern and southern edges should remain heavily forested.

The eastern and western sides should provide multiple approaches.

---

# Major Zones

## Zone A: Western Forest

**Approximate Area:** 6 × 18 squares

Dense pine forest.

### Features

- Tall pine trees
- Snow-covered ground
- Small gaps between trees
- Fallen branches
- Occasional rocks

### Gameplay

Provides cover and concealment.

Suitable for:

- Kobold positioning
- Player approach
- Tactical movement

---

## Zone B: Central Battlefield

**Approximate Area:** 12 × 10 squares

The most open area of the map.

This is where the evidence of the dragon battle is concentrated.

### Features

- Shattered trees
- Large claw marks
- Blood-stained snow
- Broken weapons
- Fallen branches
- Disturbed snow
- Several large impact areas

### Gameplay

This is the primary combat area.

It should be open enough for:

- Ysilwen
- Lotorrix
- Player Characters

to maneuver.

---

## Zone C: Eastern Thicket

**Approximate Area:** 6 × 18 squares

Denser forest with more irregular terrain.

### Features

- Closely packed pine trees
- Snowbanks
- Fallen logs
- Broken branches
- Small clearings

### Gameplay

Good location for:

- Kobold ambush positions
- Cover
- Flanking routes
- Retreat paths

---

## Zone D: Northern Ridge

A slightly elevated section of snowy ground.

### Features

- Small rise
- Pine trees
- Rocks
- Snow buildup

### Gameplay

Provides a modest height advantage.

Should not be large enough to dominate the entire battlefield.

---

## Zone E: Southern Approach

A clearer route through the trees.

### Purpose

Provides a natural entrance from the forest.

This can be used for:

- The party's arrival.
- The kobold encounter.
- Ysilwen's approach during the final battle.

---

# Terrain

## Snow

The majority of the battlefield is covered in snow.

Use visual variation rather than making the entire map mechanically difficult terrain.

### Standard Snow

Normal movement.

### Deep Snow

Small patches of deeper snow may function as difficult terrain if useful.

Keep these patches limited.

---

## Trees

Large pine trees should act as major terrain obstacles.

They provide:

- Cover
- Line-of-sight blocking
- Tactical positioning

Do not place so many trees that large creatures cannot move.

---

## Fallen Trees

Use several fallen trunks around the battlefield.

### Purpose

- Partial cover
- Visual evidence of the previous battle
- Interesting movement choices

Some should be large enough to matter tactically.

---

## Broken Branches

Scattered throughout the battlefield.

Primarily visual.

A few dense piles can function as difficult terrain.

---

## Rocks

Small groups of rocks can provide additional cover.

Keep them secondary to the trees.

---

# Battle Damage

The map must visibly communicate that a major fight already happened here.

## Required Evidence

- Shattered pine trees
- Deep claw marks
- Blood on snow
- Broken weapons
- Broken branches
- Fallen trees
- Large disturbed areas in the snow

### Dragon-Scale Evidence

Include subtle evidence suggesting that two large creatures fought.

Avoid making the identity of the creatures immediately obvious from the map alone.

---

# Cover

## Half Cover

Use:

- Fallen logs
- Rocks
- Large snowbanks
- Tree trunks

## Full Cover

Use:

- Large pine trunks
- Dense tree clusters
- Large fallen trees

Cover should be distributed around the map rather than concentrated in one area.

---

# Movement Routes

The map should have at least three viable routes between the major areas.

## Route 1: Central

The fastest route through the battlefield.

Most exposed.

---

## Route 2: Western

Moves through the pine forest.

More protected.

---

## Route 3: Eastern

Moves through the thicket.

More difficult but provides additional cover.

---

# Entrances & Exits

## Western Entrance

Forest trail.

Primary approach for the party during the investigation.

---

## Southern Entrance

Forest path.

Can serve as an alternate approach.

---

## Eastern Exit

Dense forest.

Possible retreat route.

---

## Northern Exit

Narrow route through the trees.

Can function as a pursuit or flanking route.

# Initial Encounter Layout

## Encounter

**Enemies:**

- 1 × Cultist Fanatic
- 3 × Kobold Warriors

### Recommended Positioning

Kobolds begin concealed among trees and branches.

The Cultist Fanatic should be positioned where it can support the kobolds without being immediately exposed.

### Player Entry

Players enter from the western or southern forest.

### Encounter Feel

The party should initially believe they are investigating the battlefield.

The ambush reveals that someone is still protecting or searching the area.

---

# Final Battle Layout

## Allied Forces

- Player Characters
- Ysilwen

## Enemy Forces

- Lotorrix
- 4 × Cultist Fanatics
- 10 × Kobold Warriors

### Recommended Positioning

The battlefield should have enough open space for Ysilwen and Lotorrix to engage.

Kobolds should occupy the surrounding forest and damaged terrain.

Cultist Fanatics should operate behind or around the kobold line.

### Important

Do not create a rigid symmetrical battlefield.

The environment should feel organic and chaotic.

---

# Ysilwen Movement Space

Reserve a large open section in the central battlefield.

### Minimum Recommended Space

Approximately:

**6 × 6 squares**

This does not represent Ysilwen's exact footprint.

It represents a zone where the large creature can maneuver without constantly colliding with terrain.

---

# Lotorrix Movement Space

Lotorrix should have access to:

- Central battlefield
- Open areas
- Forest edges
- Vertical space
- Potential burrowing areas

Avoid enclosing the battlefield with impassable terrain.

# Visual Style

## Environment

- Snowy pine forest
- Winter wilderness
- Natural terrain
- Heavy but readable detail

## Mood

**First Visit:**

Cold, ominous, recently disturbed.

**Second Visit:**

Chaotic, dangerous, epic.

## Avoid

- Buildings
- Roads that look constructed
- Artificial arena geometry
- Excessively symmetrical layouts
- Large decorative elements that obstruct tokens
- Text labels
- Numbers
- UI elements
- Decorative borders

---

# Generation Notes

When generating the final map:

- Use a top-down battlemap perspective.
- Include a visible square grid.
- Keep grid lines subtle and readable.
- Do not include labels.
- Do not include tokens.
- Do not include text.
- Do not include a legend.
- Do not include decorative UI.
- Keep terrain readable at normal VTT zoom.
- Preserve open space for large creature movement.
- Make trees and terrain visually distinct enough for token placement.
