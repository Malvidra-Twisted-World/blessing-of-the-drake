# DM Preparation: Blessing of the Drake

## Adventure Snapshot

**Level:** 5-6  
**Type:** One-Shot  
**Themes:** Adventurous, Investigation, Wilderness, Winterfest  
**Primary Objective:** Find Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake, before Winterfest begins.  
**Primary Antagonist:** Lotorrix the Bitter  
**Primary Location:** Pine forest surrounding the settlement

## Core Premise

The annual Winterfest is approaching.

The village normally receives a blessing from its ancient guardian, **Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake**. Villagers leave carved tokens, beaded jewellery, and coins beneath her statue as offerings.

This year, Ysilwen has not appeared.

The local druid, **Gram Iceback**, fears something has happened to the drake and asks the party to find her.

The truth is that Ysilwen was ambushed and badly injured by **Lotorrix the Bitter**, a young white dragon attempting to claim her territory.

# Quick Reference

## Gram Iceback

**Role:** Quest Giver / Local Druid  
**Race:** Orc-Human  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** 48  
**Stat Block:** Druid

### Appearance

> Gram wears thick goatskin wraps and fur boots. She stares into the forest, biting her nails.

### Personality

Gram is normally responsible for the annual offering ceremony.

This year she is increasingly stressed because Ysilwen has not appeared.

She genuinely believes something may have happened to the guardian.

### Motivation

Gram wants to:

- Find Ysilwen.
- Ensure the village receives its Winterfest blessing.
- Prevent the village from angering its guardian.
- Protect the villagers.

### Knows

- Ysilwen normally visits every Winterfest.
- Villagers leave offerings beneath her statue.
- This year's offerings are fewer than usual.
- Gram has previously discovered Ysilwen's lair.
- Ysilwen's lair is [[Pineshelter Cave]].
- Ysilwen may be temporarily hostile if approached incorrectly.

### Key Line

> "Something is wrong. She has never missed Winterfest."

### DM Note

Gram suspects the reduced offerings may have offended Ysilwen.

This is incorrect.

The actual cause is [[Lotorrix the Bitter]].

Do not reveal this immediately.

The kobolds are hiding among the branches and attempt to ambush the party while they investigate the area.

### Combat Objective

The kobolds are attempting to eliminate anyone investigating the battlefield.

### Battlefield Notes

Use the environment actively during the encounter:

- Trees provide cover.

- The damaged forest shows the scale of the previous battle.

### After Combat

- Ysilwen

- The ambush
- The direction Ysilwen fled

# Ysilwen Rescue

**Location:** [[Pineshelter Cave]]

### Situation

The party discovers Ysilwen injured inside her lair.

She is exhausted and defensive.

Nets are caught around her claws, while spears are embedded in her side.

### Initial Reaction

Ysilwen may be temporarily hostile if the party enters the cave without announcing themselves or otherwise gives her reason to believe they are another threat.

The party should have an opportunity to establish that they are not enemies.

### Player Objective

Remove:

- Nets from Ysilwen's claws.
- Spears from her side.

### DM Guidance

Let players approach the problem creatively.

Possible approaches include:

- Strength
- Dexterity
- Medicine
- Nature
- Persuasion
- Appropriate magic
- Tool use
- Creative problem solving

Do not force a single solution.

The important question is:

> How are the characters trying to help an injured dragon?

### Failure

A failed attempt should increase tension rather than automatically turning the scene into combat.

Possible consequences:

- Ysilwen becomes more defensive.
- The character risks injury from the embedded weapons.
- The party needs to change their approach.
- Ysilwen demands that they explain themselves.

### Success

Once the party successfully helps Ysilwen, she becomes willing to communicate with them.

She explains what happened.

---

## Ysilwen's Story

Ysilwen tells the party that she was attacked by **Lotorrix the Bitter** and his kobold followers.

### Information She Can Reveal

- Lotorrix attacked her in the forest.
- The kobolds fought alongside him.
- Lotorrix wants to claim her territory.
- She used an illusion to confuse her attackers.
- She escaped while they were distracted.
- She fled back to Pineshelter Cave.
- Lotorrix and his followers have been searching for her.
- Lotorrix remains a threat to the forest.

### Important Reveal

Ysilwen did not abandon the village.

She was injured before she could return for Winterfest.

This resolves the mystery introduced at the beginning of the adventure.

---

# The Ride Into Battle

Once the party understands the situation, Ysilwen asks them to ride with her into battle against Lotorrix.

### Transition

The party leaves Pineshelter Cave with Ysilwen.

They travel back toward the thicket where the previous battle occurred.

### DM Goal

This should feel like a major escalation.

The adventure has shifted from:

> "Find the missing guardian."

to:

> "Help the guardian defeat the dragon invading her territory."

### Before Combat

Give the players a brief opportunity to:

- Prepare spells.
- Check equipment.
- Discuss tactics.
- Decide who rides where.
- Establish their approach.

Then begin the confrontation.

## Special Riding Rule

Once per round, one party member may use **one of Ysilwen's actions** instead of their own.

This allows the players to actively participate in Ysilwen's combat capabilities rather than simply riding along.

### DM Reminder

Track this as a once-per-round opportunity.

Make sure the players know when the opportunity becomes available.

## Damage While Riding Ysilwen

> The party takes half damage.

# Final Battle: DM Tactics

## Ysilwen

Ysilwen has lived for centuries and should behave like an experienced territorial guardian.

She understands the forest and knows how to fight another dragon.

### Priorities

1. Engage Lotorrix.
2. Protect her riders.
3. Use her movement effectively.
4. Prevent the kobolds from overwhelming the party.
5. End the threat to her territory.

### Behavior

Ysilwen should not feel like a passive mount.

She is an active combatant and a powerful ally.

Allow her presence to make the battle feel substantially different from a normal encounter.

---

## Lotorrix

Lotorrix should behave like a dragon rather than a stationary boss.

### Priorities

1. Keep Ysilwen occupied.
2. Target vulnerable characters.
3. Use flight when advantageous.
4. Use burrowing when advantageous.
5. Exploit the battlefield.
6. Use the kobolds as distractions.
7. Retreat only when strategically appropriate.

### Personality During Combat

Lotorrix is spiteful and territorial.

He should make it clear that he considers Ysilwen an obstacle to his claim over the forest.

If the party interferes, he treats them as additional enemies rather than changing his objective.

---

## Kobolds

The kobolds are loyal primarily because they fear Lotorrix.

### Behavior

- Follow Lotorrix's commands.
- Use their numbers.
- Focus isolated targets.
- Protect Lotorrix.
- Take advantage of distracted characters.
- Retreat or panic if Lotorrix is defeated.

### DM Note

The kobolds should not fight like fearless elite soldiers.

Their fear of Lotorrix is an important part of their characterization.

If the battle turns decisively against them, consider showing that fear through their behavior.

# Combat Escalation

## Opening

Lotorrix recognizes Ysilwen immediately.

The kobolds react with fear and aggression.

The battle begins with Lotorrix and Ysilwen as the primary opposing forces.

## Mid-Fight

As the battle develops:

- Kobolds swarm isolated targets.
- Cultist Fanatics support the kobolds.
- Lotorrix uses the terrain.
- Ysilwen attempts to maintain pressure on Lotorrix.
- The battlefield becomes increasingly damaged.

## Lotorrix Near Defeat

Lotorrix becomes increasingly desperate.

His actions should communicate that he is losing control of the situation.

## Lotorrix Defeated

Once Lotorrix falls, the remaining kobolds lose the creature they fear and obey.

The immediate threat to Ysilwen's territory is over.

Transition toward the Winterfest conclusion.

# DM Golden Rules

## 1. Keep the Mystery Alive

Do not reveal Lotorrix too early.

At the beginning, the players should only know that Ysilwen is missing.

The reduced Winterfest offerings provide a natural false lead.

---

## 2. Let the Players Investigate

The adventure is not only a sequence of encounters.

Reward players who:

- Examine the battlefield.
- Follow tracks.
- Inspect the damage.
- Question Gram.
- Pay attention to the offerings.
- Carefully approach Ysilwen.

---

## 3. Make Ysilwen Feel Like a Dragon

Ysilwen is ancient.

Even while injured, she should feel powerful and intimidating.

Her relationship with the village should also communicate that she is more than simply a large monster.

---

## 4. Make the Rescue Matter

The encounter inside Pineshelter Cave is an opportunity for the party to establish trust with Ysilwen.

Do not turn every failed check into combat.

Allow the players to solve the problem creatively.

---

## 5. Make the Dragon Ride a Set Piece

The transition from investigating Ysilwen's disappearance to riding alongside her against Lotorrix should feel like a major escalation.

Give the players a moment to understand:

> They are not merely helping a dragon.

> They are going into battle with one.

---

## 6. Keep Lotorrix Mobile

Lotorrix should take advantage of his abilities and the battlefield.

Use:

- Flight
- Burrowing
- Terrain
- Positioning
- Kobold support

Avoid keeping him stationary unless the situation calls for it.

---

## 7. Show the Kobolds' Fear

The kobolds follow Lotorrix because they fear him.

That fear should be visible during roleplay and combat.

If Lotorrix falls, the kobolds should react accordingly.

---

## 8. Let the Finale Pay Off the Setup

The adventure begins with a village waiting for its Winterfest blessing.

It should end with Ysilwen returning to the village.

The final scene should connect directly back to the opening.

# Map Preparation Workflow

For every custom map:

1. Determine whether the map is actually needed.
2. Define its gameplay purpose.
3. Create the Map Design Sheet.
4. Define dimensions and grid.
5. Define entrances and exits.
6. Define terrain and important objects.
7. Define combat and exploration areas.
8. Define token placement.
9. Review the design.
10. Generate the map.
11. Add the finished asset to the campaign repository.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not generate a map directly from the location description.**
>
> The Map Design Sheet is the source of truth for the final map.
