# Story Planner: Blessing of the Drake

## Adventure Overview

**Adventure Type:** One-Shot  
**Recommended Level:** 5-6  
**Themes:** Adventurous, Investigation, Wilderness, Winterfest

### Premise

Winterfest is approaching, but the village's ancient guardian, **Ysilwen the Great Fir Drake**, has failed to appear for her annual blessing.

The local druid, **Gram Iceback**, asks the party to find her.

The party follows the trail into the snowy pine forest and discovers evidence that Ysilwen was attacked by **Lotorrix the Bitter**, a young white dragon seeking to claim her territory.

The party must find Ysilwen, help her recover, and ultimately fight alongside her against Lotorrix.

# Story Acts

## Act I: The Missing Guardian

**Primary Location:** Village

### Purpose

- Establish Winterfest.
- Establish Ysilwen's importance.
- Introduce Gram.
- Give the party their objective.

### Core Question

> Where is Ysilwen?

### Required Information

Players should understand:

- Winterfest is approaching.
- Ysilwen normally appears every year.
- She has not appeared this year.
- Gram is worried.
- The party has been asked to find her.

### False Lead

The village has provided fewer offerings this year.

Gram suspects this may have offended Ysilwen.

### Truth

The offerings are not the cause.

Ysilwen is missing because she was attacked by Lotorrix.

---

## Act II: Into the Pines

**Primary Locations:**

- Forest Path
- Bloody Thicket

### Purpose

Shift the adventure from social investigation into wilderness investigation.

### Core Question

> What happened to Ysilwen?

### Important Events

- Gram provides directions.
- The party travels east.
- They cross a bridge.
- They continue north.
- They encounter signs of violence.
- Kobolds ambush them.
- The party investigates the Bloody Thicket.

### Required Revelation

The players should discover that:

> Ysilwen was attacked by something powerful.

---

## Act III: The Wounded Drake

**Primary Location:** Pineshelter Cave

### Purpose

Reveal that Ysilwen survived.

### Core Question

> Can we help her?

### Important Events

- The party discovers the hidden cave entrance.
- They encounter the injured Ysilwen.
- Ysilwen may initially be defensive.
- The party removes the nets and spears.
- Ysilwen begins communicating with them.

### Required Revelation

Ysilwen reveals:

> Lotorrix attacked her.

---

## Act IV: Drake Versus Dragon

**Primary Location:** Bloody Thicket

### Purpose

Resolve the central conflict.

### Core Question

> Can Lotorrix be stopped?

### Important Events

- Ysilwen asks the party to ride with her.
- They return to the forest.
- Lotorrix and his forces are confronted.
- The party fights alongside Ysilwen.
- Lotorrix is defeated.

---

## Act V: The Blessing

**Primary Location:** Village

### Purpose

Provide the emotional resolution.

### Core Question

> Will Winterfest receive its blessing?

### Important Events

- Ysilwen carries the party back to the village.
- The villagers see their guardian return.
- Ysilwen blesses the village.
- Winterfest can begin.

### Final Image

The village celebrates beneath the Winterfest decorations while the Great Fir Drake returns to her role as the settlement's ancient guardian.

# NPC Story Roles

## Gram Iceback

**Story Role:** Quest Giver / Information Source

### Wants

- Find Ysilwen.
- Ensure Winterfest receives its traditional blessing.
- Keep the village safe.
- Understand why Ysilwen has not appeared.

### Believes

Gram suspects the reduced number of Winterfest offerings may have offended Ysilwen.

### Knows

- Ysilwen normally visits the village during Winterfest.
- Villagers leave offerings beneath the Drake Statue.
- This year's offerings are fewer.
- She has previously discovered Ysilwen's lair.
- The lair is known as Pineshelter Cave.
- Ysilwen has never previously missed Winterfest.

### Does Not Know

- Ysilwen was attacked by Lotorrix.
- Lotorrix wants to claim the pine forest.
- Kobolds are currently serving Lotorrix.

### Story Function

Gram provides the initial mission and establishes the mystery.

---

**Story Role:** Missing Guardian / Ally / Revelation Source

### Wants

- Recover from her injuries.
- Protect her territory.
- Stop Lotorrix.
- Protect the village.
- Return for Winterfest.

### Knows

- Lotorrix attacked her.
- Kobolds fought alongside him.
- The attack occurred in the forest.
- She used an illusion to confuse her attackers.

### Does Not Know

- How many people have been searching for her.

- Expand his territory.

### Knows

- Ysilwen survived the initial attack.
- She is hiding somewhere in the forest.

Lotorrix is the source of the conflict and the final threat.

# Fail Forward

The adventure should continue even when players fail individual checks.

## Investigation Failure

If players fail to notice a clue:

- Provide another environmental clue later.
- Let an NPC provide partial information.
- Allow another character to investigate.
- Do not make a single failed roll stop the adventure.

---

## Ysilwen Interaction Failure

If the party initially frightens Ysilwen:

- She becomes defensive.
- She warns them.
- She demands that they explain themselves.

Do not automatically begin combat.

---

## Rescue Failure

If removing the nets or spears goes badly:

- Ysilwen reacts defensively.
- The character may suffer a consequence.
- The party must reconsider their approach.

The goal is to create tension, not prevent progress.

# Scene Planner

## Scene 01: The Village

**Location:** Village / Drake Statue  
**Type:** Social / Investigation  
**Map:** Optional  
**Primary NPC:** [[Gram Iceback]]

### Scene Goal

Introduce the Winterfest mystery and give the party their mission.

### Opening State

The village is preparing for Winterfest.

Lanterns, carols, decorations, and preparations fill the settlement.

The atmosphere is cheerful, but Gram is visibly worried.

### Trigger

The party notices Gram's concern or approaches the Drake Statue.

### Required Information

The party should learn:

- Winterfest is approaching.
- Ysilwen normally visits every year.
- Ysilwen has not appeared.
- Gram believes something may be wrong.
- The party is being asked to find her.

### Optional Information

If the party asks about the offerings:

- Villagers normally leave offerings beneath the statue.
- There are fewer offerings this year.
- Gram suspects this may have offended Ysilwen.

### Quest Item

Gram gives the party:

**Shard of Amber Resin**

She suggests they bring it as an offering in case Ysilwen is angry.

### Exit Condition

The party agrees to search for Ysilwen.

### Transition

Gram draws a map for the party.

The party must:

1. Travel east.
2. Cross the bridge.
3. Continue north.
4. Search for Pineshelter Cave.

---

# Scene 02: Into the Pines

**Location:** Pine Forest  
**Type:** Exploration  
**Map:** Optional

### Scene Goal

Move the party from the village into the wilderness and establish the forest atmosphere.

### Opening State

The party leaves the settlement and enters the snowy pine forest.

### Environmental Details

Use:

- Snow-covered trees
- Cold wind
- Footprints
- Animal tracks
- Snow-covered branches
- Quiet forest sounds
- Occasional distant movement

### Travel

The party travels east.

They eventually reach a bridge.

After crossing, they continue north.

### Escalation

The environment gradually becomes more disturbed.

Characters may notice:

- Broken branches.
- Damaged trees.
- Tracks.
- Disturbed snow.
- Signs of a struggle.

### Exit Condition

The party reaches the area known as the Bloody Thicket.

---

# Scene 03: Broken Branches

**Location:** Bloody Thicket  
**Type:** Combat / Investigation  
**Map:** Required

### Scene Goal

Reveal that Ysilwen was attacked and introduce Lotorrix's forces.

### Opening Description

The forest becomes unnaturally damaged.

Trees have been shattered.

Red stains mark the snow.

The party can see evidence of a violent confrontation.

### Encounter

Enemies:

- 1 × Cultist Fanatic
- 3 × Kobold Warriors

### Enemy Setup

The kobolds hide among the branches.

The Cultist Fanatic accompanies them.

### Combat Trigger

The encounter begins when the party investigates the area or moves into the ambush zone.

### After Combat

The party can investigate the battlefield.

### Discoverable Clues

- Two enormous creatures fought.
- Trees were shattered.
- Blood stains the snow.
- Weapons were bent or broken.
- Kobolds died here.
- Large claw marks remain.
- Something large fled the area.

### Important Revelation

The evidence should strongly suggest:

> Ysilwen was attacked.

### Exit Condition

The party follows the trail toward Pineshelter Cave.

---

# Scene 04: Pineshelter Cave

**Location:** Pineshelter Cave  
**Type:** Exploration / Social  
**Map:** Required

### Scene Goal

Reveal that Ysilwen survived.

### Entrance

The entrance is concealed by an illusion that makes it appear to be a snowdrift.

### Discovery

The party notices something unnatural about the apparent snowdrift.

Possible approaches:

- Investigation
- Arcana
- Nature
- Perception
- Magic
- Creative observation

### Cave Atmosphere

Inside the cave:

- Green crystals illuminate the walls.
- Old Winterfest offerings are carefully organised.
- The cave is quiet.
- Blood traces lead deeper inside.

### First Sight of Ysilwen

The party discovers the injured Great Fir Drake.

She is:

- Exhausted.
- Wounded.
- Defensive.
- In pain.

### Initial Reaction

Ysilwen may become hostile if the party approaches carelessly.

Give the party an opportunity to announce their intentions.

### Exit Condition

The party successfully establishes that they are not enemies.

---

# Scene 05: The Wounded Drake

**Location:** Pineshelter Cave  
**Type:** Social / Problem Solving  
**Primary NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]

### Scene Goal

Build trust with Ysilwen and reveal Lotorrix.

### Immediate Problem

Ysilwen has:

- Nets caught in her claws.
- Spears embedded in her side.

### Player Objective

Help remove the restraints and weapons.

### Possible Approaches

Allow creative solutions using:

- Strength
- Dexterity
- Medicine
- Nature
- Persuasion
- Magic
- Tools
- Improvised solutions

### Failure

A failed attempt can cause:

- Pain to Ysilwen.
- Increased tension.
- Defensive behavior.
- A need to try another approach.

Do not automatically trigger combat.

### Success

Once Ysilwen is successfully helped, she becomes more willing to communicate.

### Revelation

Ysilwen explains that:

- Lotorrix attacked her.
- Kobolds fought alongside him.
- Lotorrix wants her territory.
- She used an illusion to escape.
- She fled back to her cave.
- Lotorrix's forces have been searching for her.

### Emotional Beat

The party learns that Ysilwen did not abandon the village.

She was injured before she could return for Winterfest.

### Exit Condition

The party agrees to help Ysilwen confront Lotorrix.

---

# Scene 06: Drake Versus Dragon

**Location:** Bloody Thicket  
**Type:** Major Combat  
**Map:** Required  
**Allied NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]  
**Primary Enemy:** [[Lotorrix the Bitter]]

### Scene Goal

Resolve the conflict between Ysilwen and Lotorrix.

### Opening

Ysilwen asks the party to ride with her into battle.

The party returns toward the thicket.

### Enemy Forces

- Lotorrix
- 4 × Cultist Fanatics
- 10 × Kobold Warriors

### Special Rule

Once per round, one party member may use one of Ysilwen's actions instead of their own.

### Riding Rule

While riding Ysilwen:

**Party members take half damage.**

### Hard Mode

For level 7-8:

**Party members take full damage while riding Ysilwen.**

### Encounter Objective

Defeat Lotorrix and his forces.

### Combat Progression

#### Opening

Ysilwen and Lotorrix immediately engage one another.

The kobolds support Lotorrix.

#### Mid-Fight

The battlefield becomes increasingly chaotic.

Use:

- Trees
- Snow
- Broken terrain
- Cover
- Vertical space
- Flight
- Burrowing

#### Lotorrix Near Defeat

Lotorrix becomes desperate and aggressive.

#### Lotorrix Defeated

The primary threat is removed.

The remaining kobolds lose their leader.

### Exit Condition

The party survives the encounter and Lotorrix is defeated.

---

# Scene 07: The Blessing

**Location:** Village  
**Type:** Social / Resolution  
**Primary NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]

### Scene Goal

Resolve the Winterfest mystery and provide the adventure's emotional payoff.

### Return

Ysilwen carries the party back to the village.

### Villager Reaction

The villagers realise that:

- Ysilwen is alive.
- She has returned.
- Winterfest can proceed.

### Blessing

Ysilwen blesses the village for Winterfest.

### Rewards

- 300 gp for finding Ysilwen.
- Staff of the Python if the party brings Ysilwen back to bless the village.
- White Agate Necklaces from kobolds, worth 10 gp each.

### Final Image

Winterfest continues beneath the lanterns and decorations.

Ysilwen has returned to the village.

The ancient guardian's absence is finally explained.

### Adventure End

The one-shot concludes with the Winterfest blessing.

# Roleplay Beats

Gram is anxious and distracted.

She keeps looking toward the forest.

### During the Quest

Gram becomes increasingly hopeful that the party will find Ysilwen.

### If Players Question the Offerings

Gram admits:

> There are fewer offerings this year.

She worries that Ysilwen may be upset.

### If Players Ask About Ysilwen

Gram describes her as the village's ancient guardian.

She has always returned for Winterfest.

---

## Ysilwen

### First Encounter

Ysilwen is defensive.

She is in pain and does not immediately trust strangers entering her lair.

### After Being Helped

Her attitude softens.

She becomes willing to explain what happened.

### When Discussing Lotorrix

Ysilwen becomes angry.

Lotorrix did not merely attack her.

He is attempting to take her territory.

### When Asking the Party for Help

Ysilwen is direct.

She wants the threat removed.

She is willing to fight alongside the party.

### Before Combat

# Contingencies

## If Players Refuse the Quest

Gram can explain the importance of Ysilwen's annual blessing.

If they still refuse, allow them to make their own choice.

The adventure should not force participation.

---

## If Players Want to Search the Village

Allow investigation around:

- Drake Statue
- Offering Bowl
- Winterfest decorations
- Gram

The main discovery remains that Ysilwen has not appeared.

---

## If Players Suspect the Offerings

Let them investigate the offerings.

They can confirm that there are fewer than usual.

Do not confirm that the offerings caused Ysilwen's disappearance.

---

## If Players Skip the Bloody Thicket

Do not block the adventure.

Use another clue to guide them toward Pineshelter Cave.

Possible alternatives:

- Tracks.
- Blood trail.
- Broken branches.
- A distant sound.
- Evidence near the cave entrance.

---

## If Players Attack Ysilwen

Ysilwen defends herself.

However, remember that she is injured.

If the party realises their mistake and stops attacking, allow the situation to change.

---

## If Players Cannot Remove the Spears

Allow another approach.

Possible solutions:

- Different ability check.
- Magic.
- Tools.
- Multiple characters working together.
- Asking Ysilwen for guidance.

The scene should not become a dead end because of one failed roll.

---

## If Players Refuse to Fight Lotorrix

Ysilwen can explain that Lotorrix will continue attacking the forest.

The party may still choose what to do.

Do not force them into the battle.

---

## If Players Try to Negotiate With Lotorrix

Allow the conversation.

Lotorrix wants territory and is unlikely to willingly abandon his claim.

Any negotiation should have meaningful consequences.

---

## If Players Try to Recruit the Kobolds

The kobolds fear Lotorrix.

This creates a possible roleplay opportunity.

The party may attempt to convince them that Lotorrix's defeat will free them from his control.

Do not assume the kobolds automatically switch sides.

---

## If Players Defeat Lotorrix Quickly

Let the encounter end naturally.

The remaining enemies can flee, surrender, or scatter depending on the situation.

---

## If Players Lose the Final Battle

Do not automatically end the campaign.

Consider:

- Ysilwen retreating with the party.
- Lotorrix claiming part of the territory.
- The village becoming vulnerable.
- A future confrontation.

The exact consequence depends on the table and how the one-shot is being run.

# DM Decision Points

## Decision Point 01: How Much Does Gram Reveal?

### Default

Gram explains:

- Ysilwen is missing.
- Winterfest is approaching.
- Ysilwen normally appears every year.
- The party should search the forest.
- The party should travel east, cross the bridge, and head north.

### Keep Hidden

Do not reveal:

- Lotorrix's identity.
- The attack.
- The kobold connection.
- Ysilwen's current location.

---

## Decision Point 02: How Obvious Is the Bloody Thicket?

### Default

The signs of violence are obvious enough that the party should investigate.

Do not require a single successful check to discover that something happened here.

Checks should determine:

- How much detail they learn.
- How quickly they understand the evidence.
- Whether they notice additional clues.

---

## Decision Point 03: How Hostile Is Ysilwen?

### Default

Ysilwen is defensive rather than immediately murderous.

She has been attacked recently and has reason to distrust strangers.

### Escalation

Increase her hostility if the party:

- Rushes toward her.
- Threatens her.
- Touches her injuries without warning.
- Attempts to take her belongings.
- Uses aggressive magic.

### De-escalation

Reduce her hostility if the party:

- Announces themselves.
- Shows the amber resin.
- Explains that Gram sent them.
- Offers assistance.
- Carefully approaches her injuries.

---

## Decision Point 04: How Much Help Does Ysilwen Give?

Ysilwen should provide enough information to move the adventure forward.

She should not solve the investigation for the party.

### She Can Explain

- Who attacked her.
- Why she was attacked.
- Where the attack happened.
- That Lotorrix has kobold followers.
- That Lotorrix remains nearby.

### She Should Not Automatically Explain

- Every detail of the forest.
- Every tactical detail of the final battle.
- Exactly where every enemy is positioned.

---

## Decision Point 05: Final Battle Scale

The final encounter contains a large number of enemies.

### Standard Encounter

- Lotorrix
- 4 Cultist Fanatics
- 10 Kobold Warriors

### DM Consideration

The encounter may become lengthy depending on party size and combat speed.

Use the battlefield and enemy behavior to keep the encounter dynamic rather than simply resolving every creature as a static attacker.

## Map Philosophy

Maps should only be created when they provide meaningful value for:

- Combat positioning
- Exploration
- Tactical movement
- Environmental interaction
- Visual immersion

Not every location requires a battle map.

The module's existing maps should be reused whenever they fit the scene.

# Map Generation Workflow

For every custom map:

### Step 1: Design

Create the Map Design Sheet.

Define:

- Dimensions
- Grid size
- Terrain
- Entrances
- Exits
- Objects
- Cover
- Verticality
- Tactical areas
- Exploration areas
- Token positions

### Step 2: Review

Check that the map supports the actual scene.

### Step 3: Generate

Only generate after the design has been approved.

### Step 4: Test

Check:

- Token placement
- Movement space
- Line of sight
- Creature scale
- Combat flow
- Object interaction

### Step 5: Archive

Add the final map asset to the campaign repository and reference it from the relevant session notes.
