# Session 1: The Missing Blessing

## Session Synopsis

Winterfest is approaching, but the village's ancient guardian, Ysilwen the Great Fir Drake, has not appeared for her annual blessing.

Gram Iceback asks the party to search for her.

The party travels into the snowy pine forest, where evidence of a violent confrontation awaits.

---

# Scene 01: Winterfest Without Its Guardian

**Location:** Village  
**Type:** Social / Investigation  
**Map:** Optional  
**NPC:** [[Gram Iceback]]

## Scene Purpose

- Establish Winterfest.
- Establish Ysilwen's relationship with the village.
- Introduce Gram.
- Establish the mystery.
- Give the party a clear objective.

---

## Opening Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Winterfest is drawing near.
>
> Pine-cone bunting hangs between buildings, wreaths of fir decorate doors and windows, and the village is alive with preparations for the coming celebration.
>
> Villagers carry food, decorations, and gifts through the streets. Somewhere nearby, someone is already singing a Winterfest carol slightly too loudly.
>
> Yet beneath all the celebration, something feels wrong.
>
> Near the village's stone statue of a great green dragon, a woman in thick goatskin wraps and fur boots sits upon a rickety stool.
>
> She stares toward the forest.
>
> She bites her nails.
>
> And she does not look particularly festive.

---

## Introducing Gram

Gram Iceback notices the party.

She looks toward them, then toward the forest again.

### Roleplay

Gram is visibly worried.

She is not trying to hide it.

### Opening Line

> "You're here. Good. I was hoping someone might be able to help."

If the party asks what is wrong:

> "It's Ysilwen."

---

## What Gram Explains

Gram explains that Ysilwen is the Great Fir Drake who has guarded the area for generations.

Every year during Winterfest, villagers leave offerings beneath her statue.

Ysilwen normally appears during the festival, collects the offerings, and disappears until the following year.

This year, she has not appeared.

### Key Information

Tell the players naturally through conversation:

- Ysilwen normally comes every Winterfest.
- She has never missed the blessing before.
- The village has already begun preparing.
- Gram fears something may have happened to her.
- The party is being asked to find her.

---

# The Drake Statue

**Map:** Optional

If the party approaches or investigates the statue, describe:

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The stone statue depicts a great dragon standing proudly with its wings partially spread.
>
> Beneath it sits a broad stone bowl filled with coins, beads, jewellery, and small wood-carved tokens.
>
> The offerings are clearly handmade.
>
> Some are old and weathered.
>
> Others are fresh.

### If Players Examine the Offerings

Gram explains that villagers traditionally leave gifts for Ysilwen before Winterfest.

If the players notice that there are fewer offerings than expected:

Gram admits this is true.

> "Not as many this year."

### Gram's Theory

> "Maybe she's angry. Maybe we've given her too little this time."

This is Gram's assumption.

It is not the actual cause.

---

# The Amber Resin

Before the party leaves, Gram gives them a small shard of amber resin.

### Description

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Gram pulls a small shard of amber-coloured resin from a pouch at her side.
>
> It catches the light strangely, glowing faintly like trapped sunlight beneath the surface.

### Gram's Explanation

> "Take this with you."
>
> "If she's angry, give it to her. Tell her it came from the village."

The resin is intended as an additional offering.

---

# If Players Ask About Ysilwen's Lair

Gram explains that she has encountered Ysilwen's lair before.

The place is known as **Pineshelter Cave**.

### Direction

Gram draws a rough map.

The route is:

1. Travel east from the village.
2. Cross the bridge.
3. Continue north.
4. Search for Pineshelter Cave.

### Important

Gram does not know that Ysilwen is currently inside the cave.

---

# Player Questions

## "Why should we care about Ysilwen?"

Gram explains that Ysilwen has protected the area for generations.

Her Winterfest blessing has become part of the village's traditions.

---

## "Has she ever missed Winterfest?"

> "Never."

Gram is certain.

---

## "Could she have simply left?"

Gram shakes her head.

> "Not without a reason."

---

## "Is she dangerous?"

Gram hesitates.

> "She's a dragon."

Then:

> "But she's our dragon."

---

## "What happened to the offerings?"

Gram admits that fewer villagers contributed this year.

She suspects Ysilwen may be offended.

This is not the actual cause.

---

## "Where does she live?"

Gram explains that Ysilwen's lair is somewhere north of the bridge.

She calls it **Pineshelter Cave**.

---

# Scene Exit

The scene ends when the party decides to search for Ysilwen.

Gram gives them the amber resin and the directions.

### Transition Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Beyond the village, the pine forest stretches beneath a blanket of snow.
>
> Somewhere beyond those trees is Ysilwen.
>
> Whatever kept the Great Fir Drake from returning for Winterfest, you intend to find out.

# Scene 02: Into the Pines

**Location:** Pine Forest  
**Type:** Exploration  
**Map:** Module Snowy Tundra / Theater of the Mind  
**NPCs:** None

## Scene Purpose

- Transition from the village into the wilderness.
- Establish the winter forest.
- Gradually signal that something is wrong.
- Lead the party toward the Bloody Thicket.

---

## Opening

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The village quickly disappears behind you.
>
> The sounds of Winterfest preparations fade beneath the whisper of wind through the pines.
>
> Snow covers the forest floor in an unbroken white blanket.
>
> For a while, there is little sign that anything is wrong.
>
> Then you begin to notice the broken branches.

---

## Travel Route

Gram's directions lead the party:

**Village → East → Bridge → North → Bloody Thicket**

### Bridge

The party crosses a bridge along the forest route.

Keep this section brief unless the players decide to investigate or interact with the environment.

---

## Escalation

As the party travels farther north, introduce increasingly obvious signs of disturbance.

### Environmental Details

Use as appropriate:

- Broken branches.
- Bent trees.
- Disturbed snow.
- Large footprints or tracks.
- Scraped bark.
- Blood stains appearing in the snow.
- Silence from nearby wildlife.

Do not reveal everything at once.

The forest should gradually feel less natural.

---

## If Players Investigate

Reward investigation with additional details.

Possible discoveries:

- Some branches were broken at considerable height.
- Several trees have deep scratches.
- The snow has been disturbed by something large.
- There are signs of multiple smaller creatures.
- The damage appears to lead toward the thicket.

### DM Note

These clues do not need to depend on a single successful roll.

Checks can determine how much information the characters obtain rather than whether they notice that something happened.

---

# Scene 03: Broken Branches

**Location:** Bloody Thicket  
**Type:** Investigation → Combat  
**Map:** [[Maps/Bloody Thicket]]

## Scene Purpose

- Reveal the aftermath of the dragon encounter.
- Introduce Lotorrix's kobold forces.
- Give the players their first concrete evidence that Ysilwen was attacked.

---

## First View

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The trees ahead are broken.
>
> Not merely damaged, but shattered.
>
> Snow has been churned into muddy patches of red, and splintered branches litter the ground.
>
> Something enormous fought here.
>
> Whatever happened, it was violent enough to tear apart the forest around it.

---

## Investigation Before Combat

Give the party a moment to inspect the area.

### Visible Evidence

The party can find:

- Shattered trees.
- Red splashes across the snow.
- Bent weapons.
- Kobold corpses.
- Large claw marks.
- Signs that two enormous creatures fought.

### Important Clue

The battlefield strongly suggests that one of the creatures was much larger and more powerful than the kobolds.

---

# Kobold Ambush

## Encounter

**Enemies:**

- 1 × Cultist Fanatic
- 3 × Kobold Warriors

### Setup

The kobolds are hiding among the branches.

They are watching the party investigate the battlefield.

The Cultist Fanatic remains with them.

### Trigger

The encounter begins when the party:

- Moves into the ambush area.
- Begins closely investigating the battlefield.
- Gives the hidden enemies an opportunity to strike.

---

## Combat

### Initial Position

**Kobold Warriors:**

Hidden among trees and branches.

**Cultist Fanatic:**

Positioned behind the kobolds where possible.

**Player Characters:**

Entering from the forest path.

### Tactical Notes

The kobolds should use:

- Cover.
- Pack tactics.
- Numbers.
- The forest terrain.

The Cultist Fanatic should support the kobolds rather than immediately expose itself.

---

# After Combat

Once the enemies are defeated, give the party another opportunity to investigate the battlefield.

The kobolds were clearly connected to whatever happened here.

---

## Battlefield Clues

### Clue: Kobolds

The presence of multiple kobolds indicates that the attack was organised.

### Clue: Dragon-Scale Conflict

The damage is far too extensive to have been caused by the kobolds alone.

### Clue: Ysilwen

The evidence suggests that the Great Fir Drake was involved in the battle.

### Clue: Escape

The battlefield contains signs that one of the large creatures fled.

---

# What the Party Should Understand

By the end of this scene, the players should be able to conclude:

> Ysilwen was attacked.

They do not yet need to know:

- Who attacked her.
- Why she was attacked.
- Whether she survived.

Those answers come later.

---

# If Players Miss the Clues

Do not block the adventure.

Use another piece of evidence to guide them forward.

Possible discoveries:

- A blood trail.
- Large tracks.
- Broken branches leading north.
- A faint magical residue.
- Evidence of something wounded moving through the snow.

The trail should ultimately lead toward **Pineshelter Cave**.

---

# Scene Exit

Once the party finishes investigating the Bloody Thicket, the trail continues deeper into the forest.

### Transition

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Whatever happened here did not end with the battle.
>
> Something wounded left this place.
>
> The trail disappears beneath the trees, leading deeper into the snow-covered forest.
>
> Somewhere ahead lies Pineshelter Cave.

# Scene 04: Pineshelter Cave

**Location:** Pineshelter Cave  
**Type:** Exploration / Social  
**Map:** [[Maps/Pineshelter Cave]]  
**Primary NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]

## Scene Purpose

- Lead the party to Ysilwen.
- Reveal that she survived the attack.
- Establish the cave as her sanctuary.
- Create tension before the rescue scene.

---

# Finding the Cave

The trail eventually becomes difficult to follow.

Snow covers most of the evidence, but the party reaches an area where the forest seems unusually undisturbed.

The cave entrance is concealed by an illusion.

### Initial Description

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The trail ends at what appears to be a wall of snow between several ancient pine trees.
>
> Yet something about it feels wrong.
>
> The snow is too smooth.
>
> Too untouched.
>
> And beneath the silence of the forest, you catch the faintest glow of green light.

---

## Discovering the Illusion

The entrance is hidden by an illusion resembling a snowdrift.

Allow the players to discover it through:

- Investigation
- Perception
- Arcana
- Nature
- Magic
- Physical interaction
- Creative observation

### DM Note

Do not require one specific skill.

If the players interact with the environment intelligently, reward the approach.

---

# Entering Pineshelter Cave

Once the illusion is recognised, the party can enter.

### Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The illusion gives way to a narrow passage of stone.
>
> Cold air brushes past you from deeper within.
>
> Then the darkness begins to glow.
>
> Green crystals emerge from the walls and floor, casting an eerie light across the cavern.
>
> Scattered around the cave are piles of objects.
>
> Coins.
>
> Beads.
>
> Carved wooden tokens.
>
> Old gifts.
>
> All carefully arranged.

---

# Exploring the Lair

The cave contains offerings from previous Winterfests.

These are not arranged like treasure.

They have been carefully organised and preserved.

### Visible Objects

- Coins
- Beaded jewellery
- Wood-carved tokens
- Handmade gifts
- Green crystals

### Narrative Purpose

The offerings show that Ysilwen has valued the relationship between herself and the village for many years.

This contradicts the idea that she simply abandoned the village.

---

# Signs of Injury

As the party moves deeper into the cave, they find signs that something is wrong.

### Evidence

- Blood on the stone.
- Scratched rock.
- Torn netting.
- Spears.
- Disturbed snow and debris.

The blood trail leads toward the deepest part of the cavern.

---

# First Sight of Ysilwen

The party finally sees the Great Fir Drake.

### Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The green glow reveals something enormous curled within the deepest part of the cavern.
>
> At first, you see only scales.
>
> Then a long tail.
>
> Then the shape of a massive dragon.
>
> Green scales shimmer beneath the crystal light, and the tip of her tail glows like a distant star.
>
> She is breathing heavily.
>
> Her body is covered in bruises and red scratches.
>
> Nets are tangled around her claws.
>
> Spears have been driven into her side.
>
> One eye opens.
>
> It fixes directly upon you.

---

# Ysilwen's Initial State

Ysilwen is:

- Injured.
- Exhausted.
- Defensive.
- In pain.
- Suspicious of strangers.

She has recently survived an attack.

She does not immediately know whether the party is friend or foe.

---

# Ysilwen's Initial Reaction

If the party approaches carefully, Ysilwen watches them closely.

If they approach aggressively, she becomes defensive.

### Possible Warning

> "Stay back."

She does not need to attack immediately.

Give the party an opportunity to explain themselves.

---

# Establishing Trust

Players may attempt to convince Ysilwen that they are not enemies.

Possible approaches:

- Mentioning Gram.
- Showing the amber resin.
- Explaining that the village sent them.
- Offering to help.
- Speaking calmly.
- Demonstrating that they mean no harm.
- Showing respect toward the injured dragon.

### DM Guidance

Do not reduce this interaction to a single Persuasion check.

Roleplay should matter.

A check can support a good approach, especially if the situation is uncertain.

---

# If the Party Shows the Amber Resin

Ysilwen recognises the offering.

Her attitude softens slightly.

She understands that the party came from the village.

This can provide an easy bridge into the rescue scene.

---

# If the Party Mentions Winterfest

Ysilwen reacts to the mention of Winterfest.

She knows she has missed the village's ceremony.

Her expression becomes more troubled.

She can explain later that she was unable to return because of her injuries.

---

# If the Party Asks What Happened

Ysilwen is initially too exhausted to tell the entire story.

She gestures toward her injuries.

The immediate problem is obvious:

She needs help.

---

# Scene Exit

The scene transitions into **[[05 - The Wounded Drake]]** once:

- The party establishes that they are not enemies.
- Ysilwen allows them closer.
- The party offers to help with her injuries.

### Transition

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Up close, the extent of Ysilwen's injuries becomes clear.
>
> The nets are twisted tightly around her claws.
>
> Spears remain buried in her side.
>
> Whatever happened in the forest, the battle nearly cost the ancient guardian her life.

# Scene 05: The Wounded Drake

**Location:** Pineshelter Cave  
**Type:** Social / Problem Solving  
**Map:** [[Maps/Pineshelter Cave]]  
**Primary NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]

## Scene Purpose

- Let the party help Ysilwen.
- Establish trust between the party and the guardian.
- Reveal what happened.
- Introduce Lotorrix as the true antagonist.
- Transition toward the final battle.

---

# The Immediate Problem

Ysilwen is badly injured.

### Visible Injuries

- Nets are tangled around her claws.
- Spears are embedded in her side.
- Bruises cover parts of her body.
- Red scratches mark her scales.

She is exhausted from the attack and the journey back to her lair.

---

# Helping Ysilwen

The party needs to remove the nets and spears.

Do not prescribe one solution.

### Possible Approaches

Players may use:

- Strength
- Dexterity
- Medicine
- Nature
- Persuasion
- Appropriate magic
- Tools
- Improvised solutions
- Multiple characters working together

### DM Guidance

Reward the quality of the players' approach.

A thoughtful plan should matter more than simply announcing an ability check.

---

# Removing the Nets

The nets are wrapped around Ysilwen's claws.

### Potential Approach

A character may carefully cut, untangle, or otherwise remove the nets.

### Risk

Ysilwen is sensitive around her injured claws.

A careless approach may cause her to pull away or become defensive.

### Failure

On a failed attempt:

- Ysilwen reacts to the pain.
- The character may need to stop and reconsider.
- The party can try another method.

Do not automatically trigger combat.

---

# Removing the Spears

The spears are embedded in Ysilwen's side.

### Potential Approach

The party may need to stabilise Ysilwen before removing them.

### Risk

Removing a spear carelessly could cause additional pain.

### Failure

A failed attempt may result in:

- Increased tension.
- Ysilwen recoiling.
- The party needing a different approach.

The goal is to create a tense rescue scene, not punish the party for experimentation.

---

# Ysilwen's Trust

As the party helps her, Ysilwen gradually becomes less defensive.

### Progression

**Initially:**

Suspicious.

↓

**After the party demonstrates care:**

Cautiously cooperative.

↓

**After the injuries are treated:**

Trusting enough to speak openly.

---

# Ysilwen Speaks

Once the party has successfully helped her, Ysilwen explains what happened.

### Key Information

Ysilwen tells the party:

- She was attacked by Lotorrix.
- Lotorrix brought kobolds with him.
- The attack happened in the forest.
- Lotorrix wants her territory.
- She used an illusion to confuse her attackers.
- She escaped while they were distracted.
- She fled back to Pineshelter Cave.
- Lotorrix's forces continued searching for her.

---

# Reveal: Lotorrix the Bitter

Ysilwen identifies her attacker.

**Lotorrix the Bitter.**

He is a young white dragon.

### Ysilwen's Explanation

Lotorrix wants to claim the pine forest as his territory.

Ysilwen stands in his way.

The attack was an attempt to remove her.

---

# The Kobolds

Ysilwen explains that Lotorrix did not attack alone.

Kobolds fought alongside him.

### Their Motivation

The kobolds obey Lotorrix because they fear him.

He claimed their mountain caves and forced them into his service.

### Important Detail

The kobolds are not necessarily acting out of personal hatred toward Ysilwen.

They are afraid of Lotorrix.

---

# Ysilwen and the Village

If the party asks why she did not return for Winterfest:

Ysilwen explains that she was too badly injured.

She did not abandon the village.

She simply could not return.

### Emotional Beat

This is the moment when the original mystery is resolved.

The party now knows:

> The missing Winterfest blessing was not a rejection.

> Ysilwen was fighting to survive.

---

# If Players Ask About the Reduced Offerings

Ysilwen does not blame the villagers.

She understands that the number of offerings is not the reason she failed to appear.

The actual problem was Lotorrix.

---

# If Players Ask Whether Lotorrix Is Still Nearby

Ysilwen believes his forces are still searching for her.

This means the threat has not ended.

---

# If Players Ask Whether Ysilwen Can Fight

Ysilwen is injured, but she is still willing to confront Lotorrix.

She does not want to abandon her territory.

She intends to end the threat.

---

# The Call to Battle

Once the party understands the situation, Ysilwen asks for their help.

### Ysilwen's Proposal

She intends to return to the forest and confront Lotorrix.

She asks the party to ride with her.

### DM Tone

This should feel like a clear escalation.

The party has gone from:

> Finding a missing guardian.

to:

> Fighting alongside an ancient dragon against another dragon.

---

# Preparing for the Ride

Give the party a brief moment before leaving.

Allow them to:

- Prepare spells.
- Check equipment.
- Discuss tactics.
- Decide who rides where.
- Decide how they want to approach the battlefield.

### Do Not Overstay This Scene

The purpose is to create anticipation for the finale.

---

# Scene Exit

Once the party is ready, Ysilwen leaves the cave with them.

### Transition Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> Ysilwen rises.
>
> For a moment, the ancient dragon sways beneath the weight of her injuries.
>
> Then she steadies herself.
>
> Her claws dig into the stone.
>
> Her glowing tail sweeps behind her.
>
> She looks toward the cave entrance and then toward the forest beyond it.
>
> Somewhere out there is the dragon who tried to take her home.
>
> Ysilwen spreads her wings.
>
> "Let's finish this."

# Scene 06: Drake Versus Dragon

**Location:** Bloody Thicket  
**Type:** Major Combat  
**Map:** [[Maps/Bloody Thicket]]  
**Allied NPC:** [[Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake]]  
**Primary Enemy:** [[Lotorrix the Bitter]]

## Scene Purpose

- Resolve the conflict between Ysilwen and Lotorrix.
- Give the party a major set-piece encounter.
- Let the party actively fight alongside Ysilwen.
- Pay off the investigation and rescue scenes.

---

# Returning to the Thicket

Ysilwen carries or travels with the party back toward the battlefield.

The forest becomes familiar.

The broken trees from the earlier encounter come back into view.

### Read-Aloud

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The forest opens before you.
>
> You recognise the shattered trees.
>
> The blood-stained snow.
>
> The broken branches.
>
> This is where Ysilwen was attacked.
>
> But now you are not arriving as investigators.
>
> You have returned with the creature who survived the battle.
>
> Ysilwen lowers herself toward the ground.
>
> Her eyes remain fixed on the forest ahead.
>
> Something is waiting for you.

---

# Enemy Forces

## Lotorrix the Bitter

**Stat Block:** Young White Dragon  
**AC:** 17  
**HP:** 123  
**Initiative:** +3

## Cultist Fanatics

**Quantity:** 4

**AC:** 13  
**HP:** 44  
**Initiative:** +2

## Kobold Warriors

**Quantity:** 10

**AC:** 14  
**HP:** 7  
**Initiative:** +2

---

# Allied Force

## Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake

**AC:** 19  
**HP:** 162  
**Initiative:** +14

### Key Ability

Incandescent Breath.

---

# Special Rule: Riding Ysilwen

While riding Ysilwen:

> The party takes half damage.

This represents Ysilwen shielding her riders while moving through the battlefield.

---

# Special Rule: Ysilwen's Actions

Once per round, one party member may use **one of Ysilwen's actions** instead of their own.

### DM Procedure

At the appropriate point in the round:

1. Announce that Ysilwen has an action available.
2. Allow one player to use one of her actions.
3. Resolve the action.
4. Continue the round normally.

### DM Note

Make this opportunity visible.

The players should feel like they are actively controlling their dragon ally rather than simply being passengers.

---

# Encounter Opening

Lotorrix recognises Ysilwen.

The moment he sees her, he knows the previous battle did not finish the way he expected.

### Lotorrix's Attitude

- Surprised.
- Angry.
- Territorial.
- Confident enough to fight.

### Suggested Line

> "You should have stayed hidden."

Use or adapt this line according to the tone of the table.

---

# Battlefield

Use:

[[Maps/Bloody Thicket]]

### Key Terrain

- Pine trees
- Fallen logs
- Broken branches
- Snow
- Rocks
- Existing battle damage
- Open central battlefield
- Dense forest edges

### Tactical Principle

Do not treat the battlefield like a flat arena.

Let the terrain matter.

---

# Combat Structure

## Phase 1: Clash

Ysilwen immediately engages Lotorrix.

The party deals with the kobolds and Cultist Fanatics.

### DM Focus

Establish the scale of the battle.

Two dragons are fighting while smaller creatures clash around them.

---

## Phase 2: The Battlefield Breaks Apart

As the fight continues:

- Trees are damaged.
- Snow is thrown into the air.
- Kobolds reposition.
- Cultist Fanatics support the kobolds.
- Ysilwen and Lotorrix move around the battlefield.

### DM Focus

Keep the battlefield changing.

Do not require every round to happen in the same positions.

---

## Phase 3: Lotorrix Under Pressure

When Lotorrix begins losing the fight, he becomes increasingly aggressive.

### Behavior

He may:

- Target Ysilwen.
- Target riders.
- Use the terrain.
- Fly away from immediate danger.
- Burrow when advantageous.
- Attempt to separate Ysilwen from the party.

---

## Phase 4: Lotorrix Falls

Once Lotorrix is defeated:

The central conflict is resolved.

### Immediate Result

- Ysilwen survives.
- Her territory is no longer immediately threatened.
- The kobolds lose their dragon master.

---

# Kobold Behavior

The kobolds are afraid of Lotorrix.

### Early Combat

They obey his commands.

### If Lotorrix Is Injured

Some may become visibly nervous.

### If Lotorrix Falls

The kobolds may:

- Flee.
- Surrender.
- Scatter into the forest.

Choose the result that best fits the encounter.

---

# Cultist Fanatic Behavior

The Cultist Fanatics support Lotorrix and the kobolds.

They should remain aware of the larger battle.

### Tactical Role

- Support kobolds.
- Attack exposed characters.
- Protect Lotorrix.
- Take advantage of characters separated from the party.

---

# Lotorrix Combat Style

Lotorrix is a dragon.

He should make use of his mobility.

### Priorities

1. Fight Ysilwen.
2. Prevent the party from assisting her.
3. Exploit isolated characters.
4. Use flight.
5. Use burrowing.
6. Use the environment.
7. Survive.

### Avoid

Do not keep Lotorrix standing in one place simply trading attacks.

His movement should reinforce that the party is fighting a dragon.

---

# Ysilwen Combat Style

Ysilwen is experienced and territorial.

### Priorities

1. Engage Lotorrix.
2. Protect her riders.
3. Control the battlefield.
4. Prevent Lotorrix from escaping.
5. End the threat.

### DM Note

Ysilwen is an ally, but the party should remain the protagonists.

Use her to create opportunities rather than solving the encounter for the players.

---

# If the Party Focuses Lotorrix

Allow it.

The party may attempt to end the battle by concentrating on the primary threat.

This is a valid strategy.

---

# If the Party Focuses the Kobolds

Allow it.

The party may attempt to reduce the number of enemies before engaging Lotorrix.

This can make the final dragon confrontation easier.

---

# If the Party Splits Up

Use the battlefield to make separation meaningful.

Possible consequences:

- Kobolds surround isolated characters.
- Cultist Fanatics attack exposed targets.
- Lotorrix takes advantage of vulnerable positions.
- Ysilwen may reposition to protect the party.

Do not punish splitting simply because it is tactically risky.

---

# If Ysilwen Is Reduced to 0 HP

The party's primary ally falls.

At this point, the encounter becomes significantly more dangerous.

Do not automatically kill Ysilwen.

Consider the context of the battle and whether she can survive unconscious or incapacitated.

The party should understand that losing Ysilwen dramatically changes the stakes.

---

# If Lotorrix Is Defeated Early

Let the encounter end naturally.

The remaining enemies no longer have their primary leader.

Allow them to flee, surrender, or scatter.

Do not artificially extend combat.
