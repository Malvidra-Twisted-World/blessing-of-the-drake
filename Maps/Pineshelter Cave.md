# Map Design: Pineshelter Cave

## Overview

**Location:** Pine Forest  
**Map Type:** Exploration / Social  
**Grid:** Recommended  
**Primary Use:** Ysilwen Encounter + Rescue Scene  
**Recommended Orientation:** Landscape

Pineshelter Cave is the hidden lair of **Ysilwen, the Great Fir Drake**.

The cave should feel like a place that has been inhabited and cared for by an ancient dragon for centuries.

It is not a dungeon.

It is a private sanctuary.

---

# Design Goals

The map should communicate:

- Ancient dragon territory.
- A hidden and protected location.
- A quiet magical atmosphere.
- Ysilwen's connection to Winterfest and the village.
- The importance of the offerings she has received over the years.
- Evidence that she recently returned here injured.
- Enough space for the party to approach and interact with Ysilwen.

The map should feel mysterious at first and gradually become more beautiful as the party explores deeper.

---

# Recommended Dimensions

**Grid:** 18 × 12 squares

**Recommended Battlemap Size:** 1080 × 720 px minimum

### Scale

**1 square = 5 ft**

### Play Area

Approximately:

**90 ft × 60 ft**

This should provide enough room for:

- The party
- Ysilwen
- Offering piles
- Crystal formations
- A small exploration route

without making the cave feel oversized.

---

# Cave Structure

The cave should consist of **one primary cavern with several natural alcoves**.

Avoid creating a conventional dungeon with multiple rooms.

---

# Major Zones

## Zone A: Illusory Entrance

**Approximate Area:** 3 × 5 squares

The entrance is concealed by Ysilwen's illusion.

From outside, the opening appears to be a natural snowdrift.

### Features

- Snow-covered entrance
- Natural rock walls
- Snow partially covering the ground
- Subtle magical distortion

### Gameplay

This is the party's first indication that the snowdrift is not what it appears to be.

### Entrance Width

Approximately:

**10-15 ft**

Large enough for the party to enter comfortably.

---

# Zone B: Entry Passage

**Approximate Area:** 3 × 7 squares

A short natural passage leads into the main cavern.

### Features

- Rough stone
- Snow near the entrance
- Small patches of ice
- Faint green light from deeper inside

### Purpose

The passage creates a visual transition:

**Cold forest → hidden magical sanctuary**

---

# Zone C: Main Cavern

**Approximate Area:** 10 × 10 squares

The largest area of the cave.

This is where Ysilwen rests.

### Features

- Green crystal formations
- Natural stone floor
- Scattered old offerings
- Large dragon resting area
- Blood traces from Ysilwen's injuries

### Important

Leave a substantial open area around Ysilwen.

The party should be able to approach without squeezing through terrain.

---

# Zone D: Offering Alcoves

Several natural alcoves around the main cavern contain old Winterfest offerings.

### Contents

- Wood-carved tokens
- Beaded jewellery
- Coins
- Small handmade objects
- Old gifts from previous Winterfests

### Arrangement

The offerings should be organised rather than scattered like ordinary treasure.

They are treated as cherished gifts.

### Visual Storytelling

The offerings communicate that Ysilwen values her relationship with the village.

---

# Zone E: Crystal Formation

Large green crystals emerge from the cave walls and floor.

### Purpose

Primarily atmospheric.

The crystals provide the cave's distinctive green glow.

### Gameplay

They may create:

- Partial obstacles
- Interesting silhouettes
- Natural boundaries

Do not fill the cavern with crystals.

Leave clear movement lanes.

---

# Zone F: Ysilwen's Resting Area

**Approximate Area:** 6 × 6 squares

The deepest and safest portion of the cavern.

### Features

- Large resting area
- Blood stains
- Scratched stone
- Torn netting
- Spears
- Signs of recent struggle

### Ysilwen

Ysilwen is curled up here when the party discovers her.

She is exhausted and licking her wounds.

### Position

Place Ysilwen toward the rear of the cavern.

This creates a natural approach:

**Entrance → Main Cavern → Ysilwen**

---

# Zone G: Secondary Alcove

A smaller side area.

### Purpose

Provides additional depth to the cave.

Possible visual elements:

- Old offerings
- Small crystal growths
- Dragon-worn stone
- Accumulated objects

This area does not need to contain major treasure.

---

# Terrain

## Stone Floor

Mostly natural stone.

Keep the main walking areas relatively clear.

---

## Crystal Formations

Green crystal clusters.

Use primarily around:

- Walls
- Corners
- Edges of the cavern

Avoid placing large crystals directly in the primary path.

---

## Ice

Small patches of frozen ground near the entrance.

Primarily visual.

---

## Snow

Snow should be concentrated near the entrance.

The deeper sections of the cave should be mostly clear.

---

# Ysilwen's Injuries

The map should visually support the rescue scene.

Include:

- Torn netting
- Blood stains
- Embedded or discarded spears
- Scratches on the stone
- Disturbed ground

These should appear around Ysilwen's resting area.

Do not make the injuries excessively graphic.

---

# Exploration Flow

## First View

The party sees:

- Green crystal light.
- A large cavern.
- Old offerings.
- Signs of a dragon's presence.

Ysilwen is not necessarily immediately visible.

---

## Discovery

As the party moves deeper:

They discover the organised offerings.

This should make it clear that the cave belongs to the same guardian whose statue they saw in the village.

---

## Ysilwen Reveal

The party finally sees the injured Great Fir Drake.

She is curled up in the deepest section.

The encounter transitions from:

**Exploration**

to:

**Social / Rescue**

---

# Movement & Interaction Areas

The map should provide clear areas for:

### Party Approach

A broad route from the entrance to the main cavern.

### Ysilwen

A large resting area.

### Rescue

Enough space for several characters to stand around Ysilwen while removing:

- Nets
- Spears

### Observation

Small areas around the cavern where characters can inspect:

- Offerings
- Crystals
- Blood trails
- Battle damage

---

# Combat Contingency

Combat is not the intended purpose of the map.

However, the map should remain usable if the party accidentally provokes Ysilwen.

### Requirements

- Enough open space for movement.
- No narrow corridors that make combat impossible.
- Crystal formations can provide limited cover.
- Ysilwen should have room to move.

Do not design the cave as a combat arena.

---

# Entrances & Exits

## Main Entrance

The illusory snowdrift.

Only one primary entrance is required.

---

## Secondary Exit

None required.

The cave should feel secluded and protected.

---

# Token Placement Reference

## Initial Exploration

- Player Characters: Entry Passage
- Ysilwen: Hidden from immediate view / Resting Area

## Social Encounter

- Player Characters: Main Cavern
- Ysilwen: Resting Area

## Rescue Scene

- Player Characters: Around Ysilwen
- Ysilwen: Resting Area

---

# Visual Style

## Environment

- Natural stone cave
- Snow and ice near entrance
- Emerald-green crystal formations
- Soft magical illumination
- Ancient dragon lair

## Mood

**First Impression:**

Mysterious and intimidating.

**After Discovering the Offerings:**

Beautiful and personal.

**After Discovering Ysilwen:**

Quiet, tense, vulnerable.

---

# Avoid

- Dungeon doors
- Artificial corridors
- Multiple puzzle rooms
- Traps
- Excessive treasure piles
- Text labels
- Numbers
- UI elements
- Decorative borders
- Giant crystal walls blocking movement
- Excessively dark areas that hide terrain

---

# Generation Notes

When generating the final map:

- Use a top-down battlemap perspective.
- Use a subtle square grid.
- Do not include tokens.
- Do not include labels.
- Do not include text.
- Do not include a legend.
- Do not include UI elements.
- Keep the entrance clearly identifiable.
- Keep Ysilwen's resting area visually dominant.
- Make the green crystals visually distinctive.
- Keep offerings recognisable but not individually labelled.
- Preserve enough open floor for character tokens.
- Make the cave readable at normal VTT zoom.
